# Office flavor text (rm003.sc, OFFICE_ROOM) -- the arrival line and what the
# text parser prints for LOOK and the room's other verbs. Built into text.003
# and src/officetext.sh by `node tools/gen-text.js`; edit this file and re-run
# that, not the resource in SCI Companion's Text editor.
#
# An entry's index is its order in this file, so appending is always safe;
# reordering or deleting only needs a recompile of rm002.sc afterwards.
# Plain ASCII, or curly quotes/dashes/ellipses (transliterated). Unlike a
# script string literal, a double quote is fine here.
@resource 3
@define TEXT_OFFICE
@prefix TXT_OFFICE
@header officetext.sh

[LOOK_ROOM]
A desk, a chair, a computer, a filing cabinet. A lamp, a window, a mirror, a
clock that hasn't stopped, and a door back out into the rest of your life.
That's the whole office. That's supposed to be enough.

[LOOK_COMPUTER]
The screen that ends a session and starts the next one. It has never once
asked if you're ready.

[LOOK_CABINET]
Every ending you've ever had, filed and alphabetized. It doesn't judge. It
just remembers.

[LOOK_CHAIR]
Still warm. You just got up from it.

[LOOK_DESK]
Keyboard, lamp, a short stack of books. Everything within arm's reach. You
arranged it that way on purpose.

[LOOK_CLOCK]
Running. It was running before you got here, too.

[LOOK_DOOR]
Closed. Nothing on the other side of it yet.

[LOOK_SELF]
You look like someone who just finished something. You're not sure what.

[LOOK_WINDOW]
Trees, and a patch of sky. Out there it's an ordinary day. It's allowed to be.

[LOOK_OUTSIDE]
Still there. Still growing, whether or not anyone's keeping an eye on it.

[LOOK_LAMP]
Angled at the work. It lights what you're looking at and nothing else. That's
its whole job.

[LOOK_BOOKS]
Workbooks, mostly. The first three chapters of each one are filled in. Every
one of them was going to be the one that fixed it.

[LOOK_KEYBOARD]
Worn smooth on the keys you use most. The Backspace key is nearly blank.

[LOOK_DRAWERS]
Desk drawers. You know what's in them. You put it there.

[LOOK_FLOOR]
Carpet. The path from the door to the chair is worn a shade lighter than the
rest.

[LOOK_WALLS]
Institutional gray. Somebody picked this color on purpose. Nobody remembers
why.

[LOOK_OTHER]
You don't see anything special about that.

[OPEN_DRAWER]
Pens that don't work, a stress ball, and a sticky note in your own
handwriting: "YOU'RE DOING BETTER THAN YOU THINK." You don't remember writing
it.

[LEAVE]
Not yet. It'll still be there when you're ready.

[SIT]
You settle into the chair. It isn't rest, exactly. It's close enough for now.

[LAMP_SWITCH]
Click. Off. Click. On again. Some things are easier to reset than others.

[BREATHE]
In for four. Hold for four. Out for four. The room is still here when you open
your eyes.

[WAIT]
The clock keeps going. You don't have to.

[LISTEN]
The hum of the computer. The clock. Your own breathing, if you let yourself
notice it.

[SMELL]
Old paper and warm electronics. Nothing in here wants anything from you.

[TAKE]
You've carried enough out of here already.

[HUG_SELF]
You wrap your arms around yourself. It feels awkward. Do it anyway.

[TALK]
There's no one here but you. That's allowed.

[HELP]
LOOK at anything that catches your eye, or try something else -- it's your
office, and there's no hurry. OPEN the filing cabinet (or click it) to review
your Case Files. When you're ready, click the computer or TURN it ON to begin a
session.

[FALLBACK]
That's not something you need to do right now.

[ARRIVE]
Your office. Nothing is due, and nobody is waiting on you. Look around, sit for
a while, breathe. When you're ready, the computer will start a session. (Type
HELP if you're not sure what to do.)

[LOOK_MIRROR]
Your reflection looks back at you, patient. Is this who you are today?

# The computer's session prompt for new players (returning players get
# TEXT_UI's Standard/Extended Therapy wording instead); NOT_YET_BTN is the
# way back out for both.
[SESSION_PROMPT]
The screen flickers awake and waits. Start a new session?

[BEGIN_BTN]
Begin a session (10 turns)

[NOT_YET_BTN]
Not yet
